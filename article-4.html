EU AI Act — Article 4 · AI Literacy Governance

Article 4 shouldn't end
with a training certificate.

The EU AI Act requires organisations to take measures to support AI literacy appropriate to their people, their role, and the AI systems they work with. The harder question is: can you demonstrate what you actually did?

Important distinction: VeriSigil AI is not a training provider and does not declare an organisation legally compliant with Article 4. We provide the governance and evidence infrastructure that helps organisations document, connect, and demonstrate the measures they have taken. Article 4 compliance is determined by national market-surveillance authorities.

Article 4 Governance Workflow — Live Endpoints
01
Register the person
Record training completed, competency level, certifications, role, and org_id. Issues a sealed AI Competency Passport. Every record is Ed25519 signed and offline verifiable.
POST /v1/literacy/register →
02
Create a risk-based literacy profile
Derives required literacy topics from the AI systems the person works with and their risk exposure. Provider role requires higher minimum competency than deployer role. Topics are specific to the actual AI risk — not a one-size-fits-all course list.
POST /v1/literacy/profile →
03
Record guidance and acknowledgement
The Commission says organisations can maintain an internal record of training and guidance — a specific Article 4 certificate is not required. This endpoint creates that evidence record with a sealed receipt.
POST /v1/literacy/acknowledge →
04
Connect literacy to authorization
Before a human supervisor approves a HIGH-consequence AI action, VeriSigil checks whether their literacy profile is current and their competency is adequate. Expired literacy → HOLD. This is where Article 4 becomes operational — not just a training record.
POST /v1/literacy/check-authorization →
05
Generate the Article 4 Evidence Pack
Chronological record of all governance events: literacy registrations, acknowledgements, AI systems deployed, gaps identified, and remediation actions. Present to the national market-surveillance authority or AI Office as evidence of measures taken.
GET /v1/literacy/evidence/{org_id} →
06
Revalidation when circumstances change
When an AI system, role, risk level, or operating context changes, the literacy record triggers revalidation. High-risk AI systems require revalidation every 180 days. Standard profiles every 365 days. Article 26 additional requirements flagged for high-risk AI supervisors.
Automatic — triggered by valid_until field + literacy/check-authorization
Provider vs Deployer — Different Obligations

Article 4 obligations depend on whether your organisation provides AI systems or deploys AI built by others. VeriSigil derives the appropriate requirements automatically from the org_role field.

RoleMinimum CompetencyKey TopicsArticle 26 Applies
DEPLOYER · Low risk BASIC AI basics, hallucination awareness, disclosure, verification No
DEPLOYER · High risk (HR, finance) INTERMEDIATE + Bias, human oversight, escalation, affected persons, prohibited uses No — but recommended
DEPLOYER · Critical (autonomous AI supervisor) INTERMEDIATE+ + Authorization, delegation, runtime boundaries, audit evidence, incident response Likely
PROVIDER · Any risk INTERMEDIATE–EXPERT + Documentation, conformity assessment, post-market monitoring, Article 26 competency Yes — from 2027-08-02

Note: These competency tiers are VeriSigil's governance operationalisation of Article 4 — not legal requirements. Article 4 is risk-, role-, knowledge-, experience-, and context-based. It does not prescribe universal competency tiers.
