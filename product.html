Product · Runtime control

Control the action at the point of consequence.

VeriSigil is designed to evaluate a specific AI-driven action at a declared execution boundary, bind the decision to its exact parameters, fail closed when required conditions cannot be established, and preserve evidence of the result.

Product status: bounded staging evaluation. The current candidate is in the PROVE phase. It is not a production-wide governance service or a compliance certification product.

Core workflow

01 · AUTHORITY

Re-establish standing

Check the authority and required conditions relevant to the action now being submitted.

02 · BINDING

Lock the action

Bind the authorization decision to the exact action type, target and parameters.

03 · ENFORCEMENT

Allow or refuse

Refuse when the required authority, state or evidence is absent, stale or contradictory.

04 · EVIDENCE

Verify the record

Produce a cryptographically signed record of the decision for independent verification.

Current implementation status

  • Durable validation-receipt adapter implemented in the staging evaluation branch.
  • Fail-closed execution-authorization middleware implemented.
  • Current Terry tests use synthetic fixtures and a fake in-memory receipt store.
  • API-to-Supabase persistence, process restart recovery, two-instance replay and downstream actuator enforcement remain unproven.
  • Current signing path: Ed25519. No current Z3 or Dilithium/ML-DSA execution claim.
