AI governance · Scoped evidence review

Build an evidence record for a defined AI workflow.

VeriSigil is exploring scoped technical evaluations for consequential AI actions. The work begins with one declared workflow, a protected execution boundary and explicit acceptance criteria.

This is not an EU AI Act compliance sprint. VeriSigil does not promise to make an AI system compliant in 48 hours, issue legal opinions or provide regulatory certification. No fixed fee or delivery guarantee is published here.

What a bounded evaluation can cover

  • Map the workflow, actor, authority source and external consequence.
  • Identify the exact action parameters that must be bound to the decision.
  • Define missing-authority, stale-state, contradiction and replay tests.
  • Record what is implemented, tested, demonstrated and not proven.
  • Agree a paid integration scope only after the technical and commercial dependencies are understood.
