Research direction · Not currently shipped

Interoperable evidence is a future direction.

VeriSigil's longer-term research includes the possibility of interoperable evidence exchange across independent runtimes and organisations.

Not a current product capability. This page does not claim a live federated trust network, decentralised verification mesh, production interoperability or universal agent trust graph.

What would need to be proven

  • Stable evidence and identity formats.
  • Trust-anchor discovery and independent key verification.
  • Revocation, expiry and policy-version semantics.
  • Cross-domain replay resistance and provenance.
  • Conformance tests across independent implementations.

Current engineering remains focused on the declared execution-boundary invariant. Generalization and interoperability follow only after proof closure.
