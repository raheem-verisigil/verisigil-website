Consequence-boundary infrastructure

AI systems decide. VeriSigil determines whether consequential actions become real.

VeriSigil governs consequential AI actions at declared execution boundaries, evaluates whether the specific action is still authorized under current conditions and exact parameters, enforces the result on the governed path, and produces independently verifiable evidence.

Scope: registered protected workflows and declared enforcement surfaces. This is not a claim of universal AI coverage or universal prevention of unauthorized consequences.

CAPABILITY

Cryptographic Consequence Enforcement

Enforcement tied to the declared consequence boundary, with cryptographically verifiable evidence.

RUNTIME

Runtime Authority

Authority is evaluated at consequence time rather than assumed from an earlier state.

MECHANISMS

Authority-at-Consequence

Current authority and conditions are evaluated for the action about to become real.

MECHANISMS

Parameter-Locked Execution

The governed decision is bound to the exact action and parameters being executed.

MECHANISMS

Fail-Closed Runtime

Required conditions that cannot be established do not become implicit permission.

EVIDENCE

Cryptographic Execution Receipts

Execution results can be independently verified rather than trusted only through application logs.
