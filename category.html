Category definition · Current architecture

Verifiable Consequence Boundaries

A control and evidence layer placed at the point where an AI-driven action is about to change an external system.

Current phase: PROVE. This page describes the product category and intended invariant. It is not a claim of universal coverage or production-wide enforcement.

The systems problem

An approval can become stale between decision and execution. Authority can change. Required state can become contradictory. Parameters can drift. A protected workflow needs to re-establish the relevant conditions at the declared boundary and bind its decision to the exact action.

VeriSigil's proposition

  1. Establish current authority and required conditions.
  2. Bind the decision to the exact action and parameters.
  3. Refuse when a required condition is absent, stale or contradictory.
  4. Issue a bounded authorization and cryptographic evidence for the recorded decision.

Runtime Authority

Evaluate whether the actor may perform this specific action under the current conditions.

Parameter-Locked Execution

Prevent the decision from silently carrying over to a different action or parameter set.

Fail-Closed Runtime

Unknown or unavailable required conditions do not become implicit permission.

Cryptographic Execution Receipts

Provide a signed record that can be checked independently of the originating service.

What this category does not claim

  • Universal AI safety or protection of every agent action.
  • Complete endpoint coverage or prevention of every external consequence.
  • Regulatory certification or legal compliance.
  • Formal proof execution or post-quantum signing unless separately implemented and demonstrated.
  • Downstream actuator enforcement without a tested integration.

The current implementation status and proof gates are in the controlling evidence baseline.
