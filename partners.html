Three partnership pathways

Clear contributions. Shared commercial outcomes.

We prefer specific customer opportunities and defined responsibilities over broad partnership announcements. Each collaboration begins with a qualified workflow and a written commercial scope.

01 · ENTERPRISE DELIVERY & MARKET ACCESS

Enterprise integration partners

For AI implementation firms, SaaS engineering companies, systems integrators and technology consultancies with enterprise customer access.

  • Identify customer workflows with consequential AI actions.
  • Qualify the buyer, problem, sponsor and procurement path.
  • Support integration and deployment under an agreed statement of work.
  • Earn agreed referral, integration or delivery fees.
02 · INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE & ASSESSMENT

Technical assurance partners

For independent evaluation, research and assurance organisations with demonstrable methods and complementary capabilities.

  • Assess defined evidence and reliance questions.
  • Contribute independent review within verified capability boundaries.
  • Co-design a paid assessment for a buyer-owned workflow.
  • Retain separate intellectual property and methods.
03 · CUSTOMER INTRODUCTION & CHANNEL

Strategic referral partners

For industry networks, advisors and specialist firms that can introduce decision-makers with a live operational need.

  • Make qualified introductions with buyer permission.
  • Document opportunity origin and account ownership.
  • Agree referral economics before customer work begins.
  • Support a route to a paid assessment or pilot.
04 · TECHNOLOGY ECOSYSTEM

Developer and platform ecosystem

For agent platforms, API providers and developer-tool teams interested in testing an integration path.

  • Start with the documented REST API.
  • Validate one connector against a defined workflow.
  • Keep source code and sensitive architecture protected.
  • Publish integration availability only after testing.
Commercial rule: no unpaid open-ended integration, no exclusivity by default, no transfer of background IP, and no public partner listing without mutual written approval.
