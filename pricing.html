How do I receive my API key?

Enter your email above, choose a plan, and complete payment. Your key arrives within minutes. Key format: vs-live-{tier}-{key}

What happens at my request limit?

You get an email at 80% usage. At the limit, requests return 429. Upgrade anytime from your dashboard — no downtime.

Does VeriSigil satisfy EU AI Act requirements?

Yes. VGS-024 Sovereignty Chain satisfies Articles 9, 11, 12, 14, and 22. Growth and Enterprise include full accountability chains with independently verifiable records. Enforcement: August 2, 2026.

What is the fail-safe DENY behavior?

If VeriSigil is unreachable, all SDK connectors return allowed=False automatically. The system never fails open. Governance gaps are never silently ignored.

Works with LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen?

Yes. Download VeriSigil connectors from the quickstart guide. One import, ~15 minutes to integrate with any major agentic framework.
