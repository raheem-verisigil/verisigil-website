Frequently Asked Questions
How do I get my API key after payment?
Your API key is sent to your email immediately after payment is confirmed. You can start making API calls within minutes. Key format: vs-live-{tier}-{unique-key}
What happens when I exceed my monthly requests?
You will receive an email notification at 80% usage. When the limit is reached, requests return a 429 response. You can upgrade your plan anytime or purchase additional requests.
Can I use VeriSigil with LangChain, CrewAI, or AutoGen?
Yes. Download the VeriSigil connectors file from verisigilai.com. One import, 15 minutes to integrate with LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, or LangGraph.
Does VeriSigil satisfy EU AI Act requirements?
VeriSigil's Sovereign Accountability Chain (VGS-024) is designed to satisfy Articles 9, 11, 12, and 14 of the EU AI Act. Growth and Enterprise plans include the full accountability chain with independently verifiable records.
What is the fail-safe DENY behavior?
If VeriSigil cannot be reached, all SDK connectors automatically return allowed=False. The system never fails open. Governance gaps are never silently ignored.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with no penalty. Annual plans are non-refundable after 30 days.
