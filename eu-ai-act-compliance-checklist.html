Evidence worksheet · Non-scoring

Prepare an evidence inventory for AI governance review.

Use this worksheet to organise material for your own governance, legal and assurance review. It does not calculate compliance, determine legal applicability or issue a pass/fail result.

No compliance score is generated. A completed worksheet is not a certificate, legal opinion or conformity assessment.
