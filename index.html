Five Core Governance Layers · Click to Explore

Which governance layer do you need?

Five capabilities. One enforcement substrate. Each layer solves a specific enterprise governance requirement — from authority definition to cross-domain reliance.

Layer 1 · Authority Mapping + Topology (DAME)
Do you know who — or what — is authorised to make each decision across your entire workflow chain?
Decision Authority Mapping Engine + Human Authority Topology Layer. Compile org charts, contractor authority chains, and escalation rules into machine-enforceable manifests. Map complex authority graphs (Contractor A → Manager B for Severity 2). Decision Coordination Governance validates authority across multi-step action sequences.
POST /v1/authority/map
Map authority topology →
Layer 2 · Consequence-Aware Runtime Gateway (CRG) · Core
Can your AI execute that action — and at what consequence level should it be governed?
Consequence-Aware Governance Engine. Actions classified dynamically: Advisory · Operational · Critical · Emergency. Each tier gets different governance strictness. ALLOW · DENY · REQUIRE_HUMAN in under 50ms. Watch a $250,000 wire transfer blocked as CRITICAL consequence — live now.
POST /v1/execution/control
Watch live DENY →
Layer 3 · Human Sovereignty + Emergency Governance (HSA)
How do you maintain governance and human authority — even during operational crises?
Human Sovereignty Architecture: 8 categories permanently requiring human authority — medical, employment, legal prosecution. Graduated control: Observe → Intervene → Halt. Emergency Governance Mode: Break-Glass overrides with strict cryptographic post-hoc reconciliation. Designed for industrial, healthcare, and defense operations where crises are inevitable.
POST /v1/human/authority/check
See all controls →
Layer 4 · Governance Evidence Infrastructure (GEI)
How do you prove to a regulator or auditor that governance was active when the decision was made?
Every governed decision generates a SHA-256 sealed evidence bundle — EU AI Act Art.11, NIST AU-2, ISO 42001 mapped. Independently verifiable offline. Externally attested: PoGR Certificate POGC-EXT-A7F3C2B1D9E4F508 issued by OMNIX QUANTUM LTD. Portable — survives the platform that generated it.
POST /v1/constitutional-gateway/prove
View live certificate →
Layer 5 · Reliance Resolution Layer (RRL) · Q1 2027
Can downstream systems — in different jurisdictions or domains — legally rely on your governance proofs?
Reliance Resolution Engine: translates origin governance proofs into local admissibility. Jurisdiction mapping, liability thresholds, policy compatibility. Verification proves authenticity. Reliance establishes executable legitimacy in local context.
POST /v1/reliance/resolve
