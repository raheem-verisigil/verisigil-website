What qualifies

We start with a customer problem and a commercial owner.

We want integration opportunities where VeriSigil's current, bounded execution-assurance capabilities can add measurable value to a real organisation—not open-ended experiments or general validation of another builder's product.

01 / BUYERA named organisation, buyer sponsor or qualified customer opportunity with a credible route to procurement.
02 / WORKFLOWA defined consequential action, declared integration surface and testable acceptance criteria.
03 / REVENUEA proposed paid assessment, plugin/integration fee, licence, referral arrangement or controlled pilot.

What we are open to

  • Commercial plugin and connector design for a named customer workflow.
  • Paid technical fit assessments and bounded integration scoping.
  • Joint customer proposals with clear delivery responsibilities.
  • Referral or channel partnerships with agreed opportunity ownership and economics.
  • Controlled pilots with written scope, acceptance criteria and a paid commercial path.

What we are not offering

  • Open-ended free trials or unpaid custom development.
  • General validation of unrelated builder projects.
  • Unrestricted source-code, credential or architecture access.
  • Universal agent protection, complete endpoint coverage or production-wide guarantees.
  • Exclusivity, background IP transfer or public partner endorsements by default.
Technical boundary: all proposed integrations are scoped to VeriSigil's currently demonstrated capabilities and published proof limits. We will not represent a planned connector, untested workflow or future capability as production-ready.
Commercial path

From qualified opportunity to paid deployment.

01 / QUALIFYConfirm the buyer, operational pain, workflow, decision-maker and business case.
02 / DESIGNDefine the plugin/API boundary, responsibilities, test scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria.
03 / CONTRACTAgree NDA if needed, paid scope, ownership, pricing and revenue allocation in writing.
The first step is a commercial fit discussion—not automatic access to a free development programme. Independent testing of VeriSigil's published proof surfaces remains available on the public demonstrations page.
